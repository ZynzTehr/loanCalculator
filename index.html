Financial Literacy Guide

Master key loan concepts to make informed, confident borrowing decisions.

Amortization is the process of spreading out a loan into a series of equal payments over time. In early payments, a higher percentage goes toward paying interest, while later payments pay off more of the principal balance.

The Interest Rate is the base percentage charged for borrowing money. APR (Annual Percentage Rate) includes the interest rate PLUS mandatory fees (such as origination, processing, or closing costs), giving you a truer picture of total loan cost.

Putting money down upfront directly reduces your Net Financing Principal. A higher down payment lowers your monthly payment and saves significant money in lifetime interest.

  • Mortgage: Property taxes, home insurance, private mortgage insurance (PMI).
  • Auto Loans: Sales tax, vehicle registration, dealer documentation fees.
  • Personal Loans: Upfront origination or processing fees.
  • Student Loans: Interest capitalization during grace or in-school periods.
